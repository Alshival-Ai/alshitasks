# Workspace icons

Open **Workspace settings → Workspace icon**, choose an icon and save.
The bundled pack includes Home, Layers, Cube, Rocket, Globe, Code, Leaf, Spark,
Compass, Briefcase, Terminal and Network. Choose Initial to restore the letter.

The icon appears in collapsed/expanded navigation, the workspace switcher and
the directory. SVGs inherit the theme foreground, so changing light/dark mode
keeps the icon readable without changing the saved choice.

Shared workspace selections are portal-owned and apply to everyone. Workspace
management permission and a live connection are required to change them.
The desktop keeps the last discovered choice offline. Local workspace selections
stay on this computer. Saves check a revision to avoid overwriting a newer choice.

Pack assets are original Alshival SVG artwork. To extend the pack, add a stable
ID/name to workspace-icons.json and its symbol to workspace-icons.svg. Keep the
portal, desktop renderer and service allowlist assets in sync. Arbitrary uploads
and external image URLs are not accepted.
